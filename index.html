Nuestra Historia

A dream on Broad Street

Francisco's began as a dream and became a neighborhood gathering place. Papel picado hangs overhead, the tables are set in color, and every guest is treated like family from the moment they walk in.

Every plate is prepared with pride — fresh salsas, hand-pressed flavors, and recipes that taste like home. It's the kind of cooking that turns first-time visitors into every-weekend regulars.

Serving Erie's east side and beyond: Wesleyville, Lawrence Park, Harborcreek, Millcreek, and Penn State Behrend country.
